ABOUT

THE LAST PLAYER is a free multiplayer horror game that runs entirely in your web browser, on phones and computers, with nothing to download or install.

What it is

It is a social deception game. One player in each match is secretly the Controller, who can cut the lights and fake sounds around the facility. The other players are survivors who must activate three terminals before time runs out. Because only one player knows the truth, every match turns into a game of suspicion and bluffing.

Why it exists

This project is made by one independent developer who is building it from a phone, one step at a time. The goal is a fair, replayable game where the scary moments come from the other players rather than from scripted jump scares. It is still an early prototype, and new features such as more abilities, more objectives, and fake player sightings are planned.
