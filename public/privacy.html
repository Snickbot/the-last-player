PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 2026

What the game collects

THE LAST PLAYER has no accounts, no sign-up, and no forms. The game does not ask for your name, email address, or any personal details. When you join a room, the game server gives you a random temporary ID and uses it to share your position on the map with the other players in that room. This information is only held while the match is running and is not linked to who you are.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure. You can read how Cloudflare handles this in its own privacy policy.

Cookies and advertising

At the moment the site does not set cookies of its own and does not show ads. If advertising such as Google AdSense is added in the future, this page will be updated to explain that third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to explain how you can opt out of personalized advertising.

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It contains horror themes.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the game grows. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
