THE LAST PLAYER

A free multiplayer horror game for phone and PC. One player secretly controls the facility. Everyone else just wants to get out.

The idea

You and your friends are trapped in an abandoned research facility. The lights are failing, the systems are breaking down, and one of you is not who they seem. Before each match, the game secretly picks one player to be the Controller. The others are Survivors. Nobody is told who the Controller is.

Your two possible roles

Survivor

Your goal is to activate all three terminals before the timer runs out. They are in the Laboratory, the Server Room, and the Control Room. Walk up to a terminal and hold the button to activate it. Terminals only work while the power is on.

Controller

You win by stopping them. You have limited energy that slowly recharges, and each ability has a cooldown. You cannot kill anyone. Instead you create trouble:

  • Lights Off: cuts the power. Terminals stop working and every flashlight shrinks until a survivor repairs the generator.
  • Fake Sound: plays a distant thud in a room where no survivor is standing, to split the group up and make everyone paranoid.

How a match works

  • A match needs at least 2 players and supports up to 8.
  • Each match lasts 8 minutes.
  • If the power goes out, survivors must go to the Generator Room in the far east of the map and hold the repair button to bring it back.
  • Survivors win if all three terminals are activated. The Controller wins if time runs out.
  • When the match ends, the game reveals who the Controller was.

Controls

  • Phone: touch and drag anywhere on the screen to walk. Your flashlight points the way you move.
  • PC: use W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.

Play with friends

Everyone who opens the game joins the same public lobby by default. For a private match, add a room code to the end of the web address, for example ?room=FRIENDS, and send that link to your friends. Only people with that link will be in your room.

Tips

  • Stay together. A survivor who wanders off alone is the Controller's best friend.
  • Pay attention to sounds. A thud nobody explains might not be an accident.
  • Watch the room names at the top of your screen to know where you are.
  • The dark hides other players too, so if someone steps out of your flashlight, you lose track of them.
